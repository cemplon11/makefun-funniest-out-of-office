# Candidate out-of-office lines

## 1. "Gone" (chosen)
I'm out of the office, which is corporate for "gone." My emails are being ignored in the order they were received.

## 2. Pretending
I'm currently out of office, pretending my inbox doesn't exist. It noticed. It's telling everyone.

## 3. Best life
I'm away from my desk, living my best life, which mostly means avoiding this inbox until further notice.

## Pick
Candidate 1 wins: the "corporate for 'gone'" reframe lands fast, and "ignored in the order they were received" is a second joke that rewards the read — two punchlines in one line, still well under 140 characters.
